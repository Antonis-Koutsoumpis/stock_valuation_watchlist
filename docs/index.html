Variables

Up to 4, each with its own axis.

Show an index on the graph

Drawn as a dashed line with the Price, scaled so it starts at the stock's price on the first day of the time range. The VIX gets its own axis.

Scale
Daily metrics (other than Price)

Quarterly average shows one point per quarter. The price is always a daily line; quarterly metrics such as Revenue are bars.

Trend lines

Linear: straight line. Polynomial: a curve that can bend. Logarithmic: rises quickly at first, then flattens out. Exponential: constant growth per year (the legend shows the yearly growth).

Average lines

Dotted line at the average over the last 5 or 10 years of the time range.

Calculated value

Empty: the median of the stock's 5- and 10-year growth and the analysts' estimate, between 0% and 20%. A typed rate is the middle scenario (conservative 3% lower, optimistic 3% higher).
